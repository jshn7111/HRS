# 🏨 StayEase - Complete Product Prototype

## Project Overview

StayEase is a modern hotel booking platform that allows travelers to discover, compare, and reserve hotel rooms with a seamless experience. The platform connects travelers, hotel owners, and administrators within a single ecosystem.

---

# Vision

Create a fast, secure, and user-friendly hotel booking platform capable of serving thousands of hotels and users worldwide.

---
